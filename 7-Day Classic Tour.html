The 7-Day Classic Egypt Tour

The traditional path connecting the bustle of Cairo with a 3-night deluxe river voyage down the Nile.

Day 1: Capital Arrival

Touch down in Cairo for professional luggage assistance and private check-in.

Day 2: Giza Plateau Archaeological Tour

Stand before the towering stone blocks of the last remaining Wonder of the Ancient World.

Day 3: Flight to Upper Egypt & Karnak Temple

Catch a domestic flight south to board your cruise ship, starting with the colossal pillars of Karnak.

Day 4: Valley of the Kings Hidden Tombs

Enjoy a final breakfast at your hotel before taking a private ground transfer back to the terminal.

Day 5: Sailing the Nile to Kom Ombo

Relax on the observation sun deck as the ship glides past rural river villages toward riverside temples.

Day 6: Aswan's Island Philae Temple

Arrive at the southern border to take a motorboat out to the beautiful, water-bound temple of Isis.

Day 7: Final Journey Home

Disembark in Aswan, catch your quick domestic flight to Cairo, and smoothly board your international connection.
