The Great Pyramid of Khufu

The oldest and largest pyramid; you can walk up to it or climb inside its long stone halls.

The Great Sphinx

A giant stone statue with a lion body and a human head that stands guard near the pyramids.

The Pyramid of Khafre

Appears taller because it sits on high ground and still wears its original smooth top coat.

The Valley Temple of Khafre

An old stone temple by the Sphinx used long ago to prep the king's body.

The Pyramid of Menkaure

The smallest of the big three main pyramids, built with special red granite blocks at the bottom.
