Karnak Temple

A massive complex of sanctuaries, kiosks, and towers known for the giant columns in the Great Hypostyle Hall.

Luxor Temple

A graceful temple in the middle of the city that shines brightly when lit up at night.

Valley of the Kings

A hidden desert valley holding underground rock-cut tombs built for ancient pharaohs like Tutankhamun.

A striking multi-level colonnaded structure built against steep limestone cliffs at Deir el-Bahari.

Colossi of Memnon

Two massive stone statues of Amenhotep III that stand guard near the edge of the western fields.
