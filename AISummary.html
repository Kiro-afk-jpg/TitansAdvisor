Giza Plateau

Home to the iconic Great Pyramids and the Great Sphinx, located just outside Cairo.

Cairo

Features historic Islamic Cairo, Khan el-Khalili bazaar, and the massive Grand Egyptian Museum.

Luxor

Known as an open-air museum with Karnak Temple, Luxor Temple, and the Valley of the Kings.

Aswan

A relaxed Nile-side city featuring the Philae Temple and nearby Abu Simbel.

Alexandria

A historic Mediterranean port city with Roman catacombs and the Qaitbay Citadel.
