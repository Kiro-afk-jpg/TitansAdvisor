Citadel Of Qaitbay

A 15th-century seaside fortress built on the exact site of the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria.

Bibliotheca Alexandrina

A huge modern library and cultural center built to honor the famous ancient library of Alexandria.

Catacombs of Kom el Shoqafa

A large underground multi-level Roman tomb that mixes Pharaonic, Greek, and Roman design styles.

Montaza Palace And Gardens

A stunning royal palace surrounded by nearly 150 acres of gardens and beautiful coastal views.

Alexandria Corniche

A long, lively waterfront walkway where you can see the sea, enjoy local cafes, and cross the famous Stanley Bridge.
