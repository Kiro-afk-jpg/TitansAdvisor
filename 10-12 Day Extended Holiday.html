The 12-Day Extended Mediterranean & Red Sea Holiday

Your custom route integrating Greco-Roman history in Alexandria, a classic Nile cruise, and Red Sea coastal relaxation.

Day 1: Cairo Arrival & Welcome

Settle into your premium hotel accommodations after custom airport greeting and transfer.

Day 2: Giza Pyramid Complex

Tour the massive royal tombs of Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure on the desert plateau.

Day 3: Coastal Road Trip to Alexandria

Head north to the Mediterranean to tour the underground Catacombs of Kom El Shoqafa.

Day 4: The Historic Citadel of Qaitbay

Explore the historic 15th-century seaside fortress built directly over the ruins of the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria.

Day 5: Flight to Upper Egypt

Drive back to Cairo to catch your regional flight south to your 5-star Nile cruise boat.

Day 6: Valley of the Kings Excursion

Descend into the vibrant, hand-painted burial chambers deep inside the limestone valleys.

Day 7: Relaxing on the Nile Cruise

Watch local Egyptian village life pass by from your cruise balcony while floating toward Edfu Temple.

Day 8: Aswan's High Dam & Philae Temple

Discover how the Nile was tamed at the High Dam before exploring the beautiful temple of Isis.

Day 9: Massive Abu Simbel Temples

Take an early morning desert excursion south to witness the rock-hewn figures of Ramesses II.

Day 10: Desert Crossing to Hurghada Coast

Drive across the Eastern Desert to arrive at an all-inclusive Red Sea beach resort.

Day 11: Red Sea Reef Snorkeling

Spend the day unwinding on sandy beaches or snorkeling over crystal-clear coral reefs.

Day 12: Flight Back to Cairo & Departure

Fly from Hurghada Airport directly back to Cairo to catch your final evening flight home.
