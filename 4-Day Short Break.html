The 4-Day Cairo Short Break

A fast-paced capital itinerary focusing entirely on the core monuments of Lower Egypt.

Day 1: Cairo Welcoming

Arrive at Cairo International Airport with a private escort directly to your city-center hotel room.

Day 2: The Great Pyramids & Sphinx

Spend a full day exploring the grand Pyramids of Giza, the Sphinx, and the ancient Step Pyramid of Saqqara.

Day 3: Egyptian Museum Treasures

Browse through thousands of historic artifacts inside the Egyptian Museum before strolling through Khan El Khalili bazaar.

Day 4: Flight Departure

Enjoy a final breakfast at your hotel before taking a private ground transfer back to the terminal.
